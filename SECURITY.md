# Security and privacy reporting

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities, exposed credentials, private
learner data, recordings, private imports, personal diagnostics, or other
sensitive evidence in a public issue, pull request, or Discussion.

## Report a vulnerability or privacy incident

Use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/yaron-E92/TomeOfTongues/security/advisories/new)
for security vulnerabilities and privacy-sensitive reports. Include only the
information needed to reproduce and assess the problem:

- affected version, commit, or language-pack version and checksum;
- affected component or stable content target and revision;
- impact and minimal reproduction steps;
- whether credentials, personal information, private content, or recordings
  may be involved; and
- a safe way for maintainers to request further detail.

Do not attach real learner data, secrets, private imports, or unnecessary
personal information. Use redacted examples wherever possible.

If GitHub reports that private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, contact
the repository owner or a maintainer through a non-sensitive contact method
listed on their GitHub profile and ask for a secure reporting channel. Do not
send vulnerability details in that first public or unencrypted message, and do
not open a public placeholder issue containing sensitive facts.

Maintainers route these reports to the privacy/security reviewer and any
relevant technical, licensing/content, or release owner. This document does
not appoint reviewers, promise a response time, or make a public issue private.

## General feedback is different

Non-sensitive product, lesson, translation, acceptable-answer, audio,
accessibility, and pedagogical feedback follows the public route described in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[community contribution guide](docs/community-contribution-guide.md).

TomeOfTongues is local-first. General private feedback remains local or
export-only unless the user deliberately shares it through a channel they
choose. The project does not currently promise a private general-feedback
service, account system, automatic diagnostic upload, or recording upload.

AI may help prepare a proposal after approved validation, consent, and
redaction, but AI cannot approve security, privacy, rights, language, reviewer
status, or release state. Qualified humans make those decisions.
